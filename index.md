# Privacy Policy — Bento Grid: Kawaii Kitchen

**Last updated:** July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bento Grid: Kawaii Kitchen ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App.

## Summary

The App does not require an account and does not collect or store any personally identifiable information on our own servers. Your game progress (coins, stars, unlocked levels, selected palette) is saved locally on your device. The App does use third-party services — described below — that may collect limited technical data, including for advertising purposes.

## Advertising — Google AdMob

The App uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements, including rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process data such as:

- Advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
- Device identifiers and device information
- IP address and general location information
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

Depending on your consent choices and region (including the EEA and UK), AdMob may serve either personalized or non-personalized ads. Where required by applicable law (such as the GDPR), we present a consent message before showing ads so you can choose whether to allow personalized advertising and data collection for that purpose.

For more information on how Google collects and processes data through AdMob, see Google's [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [AdMob's data disclosure](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

## Google Play Games Services

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, the App uses **Google Play Games Services** to enable features such as saving progress to the cloud, leaderboards, or achievements. This is governed by Google's own [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Play Games Services Terms](https://developers.google.com/games/services/terms).

## Google Play Billing

In-app purchases (such as removing ads) are processed through **Google Play Billing**. We do not receive or store your payment card details — these are handled entirely by Google Play. See Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on how purchase data is handled.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the information below so we can address it.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Locally saved game progress remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. We do not retain any personal data on our own servers, as we do not operate any. To request deletion of data collected by third-party services described above (such as your Google Advertising ID association), please refer to your device's ad settings or Google's own privacy controls.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
